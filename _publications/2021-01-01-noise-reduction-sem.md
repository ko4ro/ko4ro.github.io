We propose a U-net-based deep learning approach to noise reduction in scanning electron microscope (SEM) images. By replacing the conventional mean-squared error loss with the structural similarity (SSIM) loss function, the model better preserves fine structural details while effectively suppressing noise. Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed method achieves superior image quality compared to baseline approaches.
