We propose a generalized few-shot object detection framework based on frozen vision-language pretraining networks. By keeping the backbone fixed and learning only lightweight detection heads, our method significantly reduces training cost while maintaining scalability and competitive accuracy. Experiments on standard benchmarks demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach in both base and novel class settings.
